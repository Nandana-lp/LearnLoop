# 🎓 LearnLoop

### College Student Management & Academic Management System

LearnLoop is a web-based academic management system designed to connect **students, teachers, and administrators** in a single platform.

The system manages academic activities such as student management, teacher management, batches/cohorts, semester progression, subject allocation, timetables, hourly attendance, assignments, notes, leave requests, academic events, and user settings.

The application is designed with a clean, responsive **white glassmorphism user interface** for a modern college-management experience.

---

## ✨ Features

### 👨‍💼 Administrator

The administrator manages the complete academic environment.

- Administrator dashboard
- Student approval and management
- Teacher management
- Add, edit, activate/deactivate and remove teachers
- Individual student removal
- Batch/cohort management
- Course management
- Semester management
- Subject allocation
- Teacher-to-subject assignment
- Semester-wise teaching assignment
- Timetable management
- Five-hour daily timetable
- Academic calendar management
- Student and teacher overview
- Settings and profile management
- Light/Dark theme

---

### 👨‍🏫 Teacher

Teachers can manage their academic activities based on their assigned teaching context.

- Teacher dashboard
- Select teaching semester from dashboard
- View assigned subjects
- View assigned batch/cohort
- Upload notes
- View uploaded notes
- Create assignments
- View assignment submissions
- Receive submissions from the correct students
- Mark hourly attendance
- View timetable
- Academic calendar
- Profile management
- Theme settings
- Notification preferences
- Password management
